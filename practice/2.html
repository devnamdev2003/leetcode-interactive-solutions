Hardware & The OSI Model

Network devices operate at specific layers of the OSI model. Their layer dictates how "smart" they are. A Layer 1 device (Hub/Repeater) only understands raw electrical electricity/signals. A Layer 2 device (Switch/Bridge) understands MAC addresses. A Layer 3 device (Router) understands logical IP addresses.
