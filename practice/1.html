The OSI Reference Model

The Open Systems Interconnection model is a conceptual framework describing the functions of a networking system. It groups communication functions into seven logical layers. Data moving downwards from L7 to L1 is Encapsulated (headers added). Data moving upwards from L1 to L7 is Decapsulated (headers stripped).
